How to read a row

  • Schedule / when — the cadence exactly as the official source words it. A blank cell marked not confirmed means the source did not state it, so it has been left empty rather than guessed.
  • Source — a direct link to the organiser's, city agency's or state agency's page that was opened and checked on 2026-10-18.
  • verified / unverified — the badge shows whether every value on the row could be confirmed from the official source. A concluded badge means the listed edition had already finished when the snapshot was built; it stays in the list as a record.
  • Rows in italics beneath a table entry are analyst notes recording a discrepancy, a seasonal caveat or a scope decision. They are evidence, not filler.
