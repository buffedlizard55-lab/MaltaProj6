How to read this dashboard

Source first

Each row's source column names the domain that confirmed it. Government pages are used first, then the event organiser, then the venue. The full registry is on the Sources page.

Blanks are deliberate

If an official page did not state a time, date, address or price, the field is empty. It is never filled from memory or from an aggregator. Rows carrying such gaps are labelled and listed on Flagged for Review.

City boundary enforced

Only events in San Francisco are included. Oakland's Black Joy Parade, Daly City's street festival and Levi's Stadium events in Santa Clara are excluded on purpose; the exclusions are documented in the review log.

Scope: this is a broad catalogue of public events, not a ticketing feed. Private and members-only events, school-only programmes and one-off private venue bookings are out of scope. Venue concert calendars are represented by their published seasons and headline runs, not by every individual performance. Read the method and limitations →
