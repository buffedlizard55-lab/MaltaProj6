Annual events

Traditions that return every year: parades, street fairs, festivals, races and cultural seasons. 28 entries are catalogued. 14 already have official 2027 dates; the rest show the most recent confirmed edition and state plainly when the next edition has not been published.

Dates are edition-specific. An annual event's month can shift with the lunar calendar, the last Sunday rule, or the organiser's permits. The Dates column always names the edition the date belongs to. “2027 not published” means the organiser had not announced it on the page checked on 2026-10-18; it does not mean the event is cancelled.
