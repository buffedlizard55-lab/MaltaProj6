What the 2026-10-18 final pass changed

  • Removed an out-of-scope row. The California Museum was listed as a San Francisco attraction at "1045 Moscone Center". The museum's own pages place it at 1020 O Street, Sacramento, with $10 adult admission. The row was deleted; the evidence is on the Flagged for review page.
  • Corrected the Conservatory of Flowers. It is closed Wednesdays, opens 10:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. with last entry at 4:00 p.m., and the first Tuesday of the month is free. The row previously said "open daily".
  • Removed Battery Spencer. The National Park Service lists it in the Marin Headlands, outside the City and County of San Francisco.
  • Filled verified gaps. SF Music Week (Feb 23-Mar 1, 2026), the Parol Lantern Festival (Dec 12, 2026, 24th annual), RSAC 2027 (Apr 5-8, 2027) and Noise Pop 2027 (from Feb 18, 2027) now carry dates taken from their organisers' pages.
  • Reclassified TreasureFest. The organiser describes a monthly festival and publishes no 2026 dates, so it is no longer labelled "Annual" and its dates stay blank.
  • Closed four long-standing gaps. FOG Design+Art (gala Jan 20, fair Jan 21-24, 2027, Fort Mason, with the advance and at-door prices), Portola Music Festival (Sep 26-27, 2026, Pier 80, Goldenvoice), the Juneteenth Fillmore celebration (Jun 13, 2026, 11 a.m.-6 p.m., free) and the Golden State Valkyries (2026 home opener May 10; Finals games Oct 17 and 19) are now sourced to the organiser's own page.
  • Added three verified rows. The San Francisco Mime Troupe's free summer season (dated from the company's official calendar feed), the Yerba Buena Gardens Festival (2026 season opener May 9, 100+ free performances May-November) and the Conservatory of Flowers light show (nightly, 30 minutes after sunset, Oct 1, 2026-Jan 30, 2027, free).
  • Marked concluded editions. 42 rows describe an edition that had already finished on 2026-10-18. They stay in the catalogue as records and carry a "concluded" badge, so the list is not mistaken for an upcoming-events feed.
  • Reduced the review gap. Re-checking official pages against the published values took the unverified count from 40 to 76. The remaining gaps are listed row by row on the review page.

How to read this

  • "Verified" means every populated cell on the row matches the official page linked in its Source column on the date recorded. A blank cell means the source did not state that value.
  • "Needing review" means at least one populated value could not be confirmed, official pages conflict, or the event's current status is uncertain.
  • Counts are a snapshot. San Francisco publishes hundreds of events a week; this catalogue is a curated, source-linked inventory, not a live calendar. See Method.

San Francisco Events — A curated, source-linked snapshot of public events in San Francisco.

Each listed entry links to the organiser, city agency or state source recorded as checked on 2026-10-18. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and marked for review. This is a curated snapshot, not an exhaustive or live events calendar; confirm details with the official organiser before attending. No values are inferred.

Scope is the City and County of San Francisco only. Events physically located in other Bay Area cities are excluded and the exclusions are listed on the Flagged for review page.
