## 1. Method

1. Read the published `data/events.json` (216 rows + 89 review items) and all 14 generated pages.
2. Ran an independent structural checker (`audit/check_8e8e24dc.py`, new file) over every row and every
   internal link. Result: `hard_problems=0`; 68 rows carry at least one heuristic finding.
3. Re-fetched **29 official pages** on 2026-10-18 and compared the published values field by field. The
   list of pages is in §3. Aggregators and search snippets were used only to **locate** official pages,
   never as evidence; every verdict below rests on the official page text quoted.
4. Left every unconfirmed value as it was. Nothing was inferred or filled in.

The checker does not fetch pages; this report is the source-text record. Commands:

```bash
python3 audit/check_8e8e24dc.py
python3 scripts/build_site.py      # reproducibility check only; no edits
python3 tools/validate.py
```

`scripts/build_site.py` regenerated the site byte-for-byte (no working-tree diff) and
`tools/validate.py` returned `OK` (216 / 176 / 40 / 89 / 95 domains / 14 pages), so the published tree is
internally reproducible. Structural consistency is **not** factual accuracy; §2 lists what is wrong.

## 4. Past-dated editions (38 rows)

The dataset is a snapshot taken 2026-10-18, but the site does not distinguish **upcoming** from **most recent
(past)** editions. The checker found 38 rows whose latest published date is before today, e.g.:

- **Specials that have ended:** Flower Piano (Sep 20), Portola (Sep 27), Opera in the Park (Sep 13), Gala for
  the Gardens (May 9), Fort Point Living History (Aug 1), NYE (Jan 1), Presidio summer season (Sep 6),
  Dreamforce (Sep 17), Frameline50 Fall Showcase (Nov 5 end — actually still running), Brava Block Party
  (Oct 17), No Kings Rally (Oct 17).
- **Annuals showing the 2026 edition:** Outside Lands (Aug 9), Juneteenth Fillmore (Jun 13), CAAMFest (May 10),
  Silent Film (May 10), Dragon Boat (Jun 21), SFFILM (May 4), Castro Street Fair (Oct 4), Folsom (Sep 27),
  Autumn Moon (Sep 20), Nihonmachi (Aug 2), North Beach (Jun 21), Union Street (Jun 7), Haight (Sep 20),
  Italian Heritage (Oct 11), Fleet Week (Oct 11), St Patrick's (Mar 14), CNY Flower Market (Feb 15),
  Trans March (Jun 26), Fourth of July (Jul 4), SF Open Studios (Oct 25 end — still running).
- **Recurring rows whose 2026 season ended:** Illuminate LIVE, Free Shakespeare, Summer Stride.

Some of these are legitimately "most recent edition" records, but the pages present them identically to
upcoming events. The site should add an explicit **"Past edition"** badge (or split upcoming vs archive), and
the Special page blurb must stop implying that every listed date is upcoming.

The checker's past-date list is a heuristic: multi-day ranges that end after today (e.g. SF Open Studios to
Oct 25) and rows with blank dates are not counted as past.
