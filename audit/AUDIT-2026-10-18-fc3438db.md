## 3. Corrections to existing rows

1. **spc-boo-at-zoo — cost corrected.** The Zoo's own page states "Boo at the Zoo activities are free
   with paid daytime admission or Zoo membership". The row previously said `Ticketed`; it now reads
   `Free with paid zoo admission or Zoo membership`, and the source was upgraded from the Rec & Park
   calendar mirror to https://www.sfzoo.org/event/boo-at-the-zoo/ (the Rec & Park calendar URL is kept
   in the note).
2. **ann-sf-mime-troupe — source upgraded.** The row's source_url pointed at the company's Google-hosted
   iCal feed (`calendar.google.com/...`), which the structural checker treats as a secondary host. The
   organiser's own page https://www.sfmt.org/summer-schedule (fetched 2026-10-18, links that feed as
   "Download iCal Calendar") is now the primary source; the feed URL remains in the note as evidence.

## 4. Site alignment with the stated requirements

1. **Executive summary near today's date** — new `exec-summary` section on the home page: dated rows
   starting within three weeks of the snapshot (soonest first) and rows running now, each with cost
   labels and official links; counts stated in prose. Dates are parsed from the printed strings only
   (`parse_dates()`, presentation-only; fuzzy wording like "mid-November" is never resolved).
2. **Organized by date, upcoming first** — the home page "Upcoming dated events" table previously
   listed the first 8 special rows in dataset order (Boo at the Zoo appeared before Scaregrove, etc.).
   It is now drawn from all dated rows, sorted soonest-first, with a Cost column.
3. **Clear cost labels** — every event table's Cost cell now carries a Free / $ / Free-paid chip
   beside the source-worded value; blanks render as "not confirmed". The full list gains a
   **Cost filter** (Free / Paid / Mixed / Not confirmed).
4. **Free focus: gaming, music, AI** — a "Free spotlight" table on the home page groups the free
   (or partly free) entries under Gaming / Music & stage / AI & tech, and the new SFPL programme rows
   fill the previously empty gaming/AI coverage. Everything else remains in the full list.
5. **Mobile friendly** — the header nav already wraps; small-screen rules tightened (stat tiles,
   2-column tiles, single-column two-col blocks, compact nav, tighter page padding). Wide tables
   stay horizontally scrollable inside `.table-scroll`, as before.
