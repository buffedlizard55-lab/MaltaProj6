**Session:** `arena-bdaa17b4` · **Branch:** `arena/bdaa17b4-maltaproj6` · **Date:** 2026-10-18

This session reviewed the work of the three earlier sessions line by line, re-fetched official
sources, researched gaps, integrated the fixes, rebuilt the site and merged it. Earlier
sessions had already published a 205-row union dataset (verified only structurally — the
previous session's own log states its validator "does not fetch or authenticate the 205

official pages themselves.

**168 fully verified rows**, **43 rows with a published gap** (after merging session `arena-7d8015f4`'s integrity pass, which set the blank-cost Alcatraz closure row to `unverified`; this session's integration alone was 169/42), **83 flagged-for-review items**.
Nothing below is inferred: every factual claim names the official page fetched on 2026-10-18,
or is explicitly marked as not checked.

## 2. Method and tool limits (read before trusting any single row)

1. **Structural pass (all rows).** `audit/check_dataset.py` checks every row of
   `data/events.json` for: source present and `https`, duplicate ids/titles, blank fields vs
   `status`, out-of-SF wording, dated rows whose latest parseable date precedes 2026-10-18,
   near-duplicate titles, unverified rows' presence in the flagged register, broken internal
   links, and each source URL's appearance in the generated HTML. Output:
   `audit/row-audit.csv` (one line per event).
2. **Live pass (stratified sample + all irregular rows).** The sandbox shell cannot reach
   official websites (verified: `curl https://www.sf.gov/` returns `000`; only GitHub/npm/PyPI
   hosts are reachable from bash), so live checks used the page-fetch tool against the
   official URL itself. 22 official pages were fetched and compared field by field (§4).
   JavaScript-shell pages (e.g. sfrecpark.org programme pages) return only their shell to
   the fetcher; where that happened the audit records "content not re-verified" instead of
   guessing. **The remaining ~120 source URLs were not re-fetched this session** and still
   rely on the earlier sessions' recorded checks.
3. **Gap pass.** Candidate missing events were located via search, but every published
   field comes from the fetched official organiser or city page. Full trail:
   `data/gap-candidates-bdaa17b4.json`.
4. **Integration.** All dataset edits were made by `audit/apply_integration_bdaa17b4.py`
   (exact-match assertions, kept in the repo), followed by
   `python3 scripts/build_site.py`, `python3 tools/validate.py` and a fresh structural audit.

## 5. Irregularities found and fixed

1. **Angel Island State Park was out of scope and its justification note was wrong.**
   The note claimed "the island and its ferry landing are part of the City of San Francisco".
   California State Parks' own facility listing gives "Angel Island, **Tiburon, CA 94920**",
   "**County: Marin**", mailing address "P.O. Box 318, Tiburon, CA 94920"
   (https://dbw.parks.ca.gov/BoatingFacilities/f/420). Under the project's city-limits-only
   rule the row was **removed** from the event list and published on `flagged.html`
   (`flag-angel-island-scope`), the same treatment earlier sessions gave Levi's Stadium.
   *Open question for a future audit:* `art-golden-gate-bridge` mentions Battery Spencer in
   its title/location; that location's jurisdiction was **not** verified this session.
2. **Illuminate LIVE counted twice** (weekly row via Rec & Park; annual row via Mayor's
   Office — identical season, venue, cadence, "125+ performances", Mar 1–mid-Nov 2026).
   Kept the weekly row; removed `ann-illuminate-live`; Mayor's release now cited in the
   kept row's notes (`flag-illuminate-duplicate`).
3. **Trans March counted twice** (annual organiser row already carried Friday Jun 26, 2026;
   the special row repeated that edition from SFMTA's reroute notice). Kept the annual row;
   removed the special; SFMTA page cited in notes (`flag-trans-march-duplicate`).
4. **Parol Lantern Festival** published a 2025 date as prose inside *Confirmed dates* while
   its note claimed the field was blank. Date cell blanked; organiser re-check recorded
   (`flag-parol-dates-cell`).
5. **Opera in the Park** claimed its venue "was not stated" in the cited SF Opera release.
   The release states the "free, annual OPERA IN THE PARK concert (Sunday, September 13)"
   at "**Robin Williams Meadow** in San Francisco's Golden Gate Park". Row reclassified to
   **Annual**, venue filled, status → verified; the unconfirmed 1:30 p.m. time and Chronicle
   presenter credit were removed because neither appears in the fetched release text
   (`flag-opera-park-venue`).
6. **Manon start date conflicted inside the dataset itself:** imported row `x-s-020`
   (source: sfopera.com homepage) said Oct 18–Nov 1, 2026; the Opera's season announcement
   and the dataset's own season row say **Oct 15–Nov 1, 2026**. Corrected in the published
   union via a documented `AUDIT_CORRECTIONS` entry in `scripts/merge_parallel.py` —
   the parallel session's raw `data/special.json` is deliberately **not** rewritten
   (`flag-Manon-date`).
7. **Rec & Park Operations Committee** published "Monthly, 2:00 p.m." while its note said
   the cadence was unstated/blank, and its "next listed" Oct 1, 2026 date is past; the cited
   entry now redirects to a CivicPlus login. Schedule blanked to match the note
   (`flag-recpark-ops-source`). Same stale "(next listed)" label fixed on `civ-hrc`
   (Oct 8, 2026 was already past at both verification passes).
8. Minor prose: TreasureFest's "All fields left blank" now reads "Schedule, dates and cost
   left blank" (its location and organiser were always filled).

Every fix above also appears as a `FLAGGED` item on the public **Flagged for Review** page,
per the project's rule that irregularities are published, never silently resolved.

## 9. What this audit does **not** claim

* It does **not** prove the catalogue exhaustive. The site itself now says "curated
  snapshot" (earlier session `arena-f54a124f`), and §7 shows why: major annual events were
  still missing until this pass, and more remain unchecked.
* ~120 source URLs were not re-fetched this session (§2). SF Rec & Park, Legistar and other
  app-style official pages in particular need a browser-based re-check that this sandbox
  cannot perform (their content did not render to the fetcher).
* Re-verification proves each cited page said what the row says **on 2026-10-18**; schedules
  change, and SFMTA "travel update" pages explicitly archive themselves as older content.
