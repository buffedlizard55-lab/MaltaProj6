### Assessment Appeals Board time conflict

The Assessment Appeals Board department page says hearings are held Monday–Friday in two daily sessions. Its current October 19, 2026 calendar lists Boards 1 and 2 at 9:30 a.m. and Board 3 at 1:30 p.m. The citywide SF.gov calendar instead lists Boards 1 and 2 at 12:30 p.m. and Board 3 at 4:30 p.m. These are conflicting official pages. The row does not copy the Board of Appeals' 5 p.m. time, and specific Assessment Appeals Board session times remain unpublished in the row until the City reconciles them. The discrepancy is visible on the Flagged for Review page.
